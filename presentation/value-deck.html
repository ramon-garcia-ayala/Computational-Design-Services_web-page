Hand them the laptop. This slide works when they move the sliders, not when you do. The moment they type their own rate, the number stops being yours.
Set the build investment live, in the room. Nothing is pre-quoted, on purpose.
Defend the assumptions before they're questioned: 80% not 100% (there is always an exception path), 46 weeks not 52, maintenance charged against us rather than hidden. A model that flatters itself falls over at the first engineer's question — and the engineer decides.
If payback lands past 24 months, say so and walk it back. "On these numbers I wouldn't build this yet." That sentence sells more work than any slide here.
